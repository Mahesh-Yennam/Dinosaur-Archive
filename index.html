Fossil Records Active in this Geologic Slice:

252 – 201 Ma

Triassic: The Genesis

Following the catastrophic Permian-Triassic extinction ("The Great Dying"), early bipedal archosaurs adapt to arid Pangea deserts, giving rise to Coelophysis and Herrerasaurus.

201 – 145 Ma

Jurassic: Golden Age of Giants

Pangea splits into Laurasia and Gondwana. Humid greenhouse climates nurture vast conifer forests, spawning titanic sauropods (Brachiosaurus) and plated stegosaurs.

145 – 66 Ma

Cretaceous: Evolutionary Peak & Impact

Flowering plants emerge. Tyrannosaurs and ceratopsians evolve specialized hyper-weapons, cut short 66 Ma by a 10km asteroid impact in the Yucatán peninsula.
